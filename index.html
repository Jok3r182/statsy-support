What do I need to connect? Either a Google account with Google Analytics for Firebase and AdMob, or an App Store Connect API key. One is enough; connect both to see users and all earnings. How do I create an App Store Connect API key? In App Store Connect open Users and Access, then Integrations, then App Store Connect API. Create a key with Sales access (Finance or Admin role), download the .p8 file, and paste the Issuer ID, Key ID, your vendor number (Payments and Financial Reports) and the key into Statsy. Why is Google sign-in blocked? Statsy uses your own Google Cloud OAuth client. Create an iOS client for bundle ID lt.matas.statsy, enable the Google Analytics Admin, Analytics Data and AdMob APIs, and add your Google account as a test user on the OAuth consent screen. The in-app steps walk you through it. Why does App Store show yesterday? Apple publishes sales reports a day late. Statsy shows the latest reported day together with its date. Why are some apps missing from the totals? Apps switched off in Choose Apps leave the list and every total. On the free plan Statsy tracks your top 2 apps by all-time earnings; Statsy Pro shows every app. How do I restore Statsy Pro? Open the Statsy Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is supported. Notifications don't arrive Check that notifications are allowed for Statsy in iOS Settings and that Background App Refresh is on. iOS decides when background checks run, usually a few times a day. Where are my keys stored? Only in your iPhone's Keychain. There is no Statsy server. See the privacy policy. © 2026 Matthew Productions
